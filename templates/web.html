    What is public speaking?

    Public speaking is an activity where a person talks in front of an audience. This connects to 4 of the main goals of public speaking. The first one is to inform someone about something. Public speaking is definitely to inform the audience that you want to share some information. The second one is to convince the audience of something. For example, if you’re doing some marketing presentation, you will convince the audience to buy the product. You will share the pros and cons of your product, and convince the audience to buy them. The third one is to entertain the audience, for example, like an MC, they will try to entertain the audience, while actually trying to also inform the audience and share something to the audience. and the last one, is to inspirate the audience. The mc will inspirate the audience, and so with that, the audience get some knowledge and will to be the better version of themselves.

    Overcoming the fear & Building Confidence

    Everyone feels nervous about speaking in public, and it is a very common challenge. With the right mindset and consistent practice, this fear can be overcome. Believing in your ability to improve is the first step, because public speaking is a valuable skill that many people will need in their future careers. Well, to overcome it, there's a way that you can do. Like, know your material to share, and understand your audience. try to focus that the goal when public speaking is to share some information, not searching for a mistake. People can say that you've been scared of public speaking since you were born, but if you let it slide, then, will it just go away? Instead, you will be the same. You will be in an endless cycle, where you won't be improving. To build confidence, you could do some exercise like talking in front of your mirror, and write some mini speeches. Honestly, there's many ways to be better and overcome fear. But in the end, it will be back to all of us. Will we have the willingness to overcome the fear? or just remain the same?

    Audience Engagement

    since we have already talked about overcoming fear and building confidence, now lets talk about audience engagement. Connecting with your audience is just as important as the words you say. Effective public speakers build engagement by making eye contact, using gestures, and showing genuine interest in their listeners. Asking questions, sharing relatable examples, or using a touch of humor can make the audience feel involved. When people feel connected, they are more likely to listen attentively and remember your message.

    Voice Control & Clarity

    Once you have their attention, your voice becomes your greatest tool. A strong, clear voice makes your message impossible to ignore. Learn to control your breathing, vary your tone, and speak at a steady pace so that every word lands with impact. Clarity is not about using complicated words, but about making your ideas simple and easy to understand. With control and precision, your voice can carry confidence.

    Mistakes to avoid & Tips

    Even with preparation, many speakers fall into habits that weaken their presence. Speaking too fast, filling every pause with “um,” or avoiding eye contact can instantly break the connection you’ve worked so hard to build. Overloading slides or relying too much on notes can also make your delivery less engaging. Remember this. Mistakes happen, but the real mistake is not learning from them. By being aware and practicing consistently, you can step on stage with confidence and leave a lasting impression.
